Meet the cell organelles

Each organelle has a specialised role that helps the cell stay alive.

🧠
NucleusStores DNA and helps control cell activities.
⚡
MitochondriaRelease usable energy through cellular respiration.
🛡️
Cell membraneControls what enters and leaves the cell.
🏭
RibosomesBuild proteins using instructions carried by mRNA.
📦
Golgi apparatusModifies, sorts and packages proteins and lipids.
🫧
CytoplasmJelly-like material where many cell processes occur.

Why are organelles important?

A cell is organised into specialised parts. These structures work together: the nucleus contains genetic instructions, ribosomes make proteins, and mitochondria help supply energy. The cell membrane helps maintain a stable internal environment.

Try this analogy

A cell can be compared to a small city: the nucleus is like a control centre, mitochondria are like power stations, and the membrane is like a security boundary. The analogy is useful for learning, but organelles are biological structures—not actual computer parts.
